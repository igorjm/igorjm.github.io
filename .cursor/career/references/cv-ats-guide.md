# CV & ATS Guide — Senior Engineer (8+ YOE)

Reference for resume tailoring. Primary PDF: `web/public/resume/igor_melo_frontend_engineer.pdf`

## ATS fundamentals

- **Format:** Single column, standard fonts, no tables/graphics in body text
- **File:** PDF preferred; ensure text is selectable (not image-only)
- **Sections:** Summary → Experience → Skills → Education → (optional) Projects
- **Keywords:** Mirror job description terms naturally in bullets and skills — no keyword stuffing
- **Length:** 2 pages acceptable at 8+ years if page 1 is high-impact

## Bullet formula

```
[Action verb] + [what you built/did] + [tech/scope] + [metric/outcome]
```

**Strong verbs:** Designed, Built, Led, Reduced, Delivered, Implemented, Optimized, Mentored, Shipped, Refactored

**Example:**
> Refactored processing pipeline for investigative analytics platform (Java, React), reducing processing times by 25% while maintaining JasperReports and Bold Report integrations.

## Experience section rules

- Reverse chronological
- **Current role (Cognyte):** 4–6 bullets — most detail
- **Prior roles:** 3–4 bullets each, tapering for older roles
- Include company context in first bullet if employer is not well known
- Align job title with target role (Senior Software Engineer vs Frontend Engineer)

## Summary paragraph (top of CV)

3–4 lines:

1. Seniority + years + specialty
2. Domain proof (analytics, healthcare, EdTech)
3. Stack headline
4. Optional: target alignment ("Seeking senior full-stack roles in product-led companies")

**EN example:**
> Senior Software Engineer with 8+ years building full-stack product across investigative analytics, healthcare, and EdTech. Expertise in Java, Spring Boot, React, and TypeScript with a track record of measurable impact — 25% performance gains, hackathon products adopted globally, and AI-powered side projects. Based in Florianópolis, Brazil; open to remote collaboration.

## Tailoring to a job description

1. Extract must-have keywords (stack, domain, seniority signals)
2. Map each JD requirement to a STAR story from `achievements.md`
3. Rewrite Cognyte bullets first — most relevant for senior roles
4. Add/adjust summary to mirror role title
5. Move matching skills to top of each group
6. Flag gaps honestly — don't claim unlisted tech

## EN vs PT-BR CV conventions

| Element | EN | PT-BR |
|---------|----|----|
| Title | Resume / CV | Currículo |
| Summary | "Professional Summary" | "Resumo Profissional" |
| Date format | Feb 2022 — Present | Fev 2022 — Presente |
| Formality | Concise, impact-led | Slightly more formal acceptable |
| Photo | Usually omitted (US/global) | Sometimes included in BR — user preference |
| Length | 1–2 pages | 1–2 pages |

Keep **facts identical** across languages; translate tone and section headers only.
