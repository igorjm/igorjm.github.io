## What actually drives payout (not just eligibility)

From [X Help](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/creator-revenue-sharing):

- **Verified Home Timeline impressions** carry the most weight
- Views from **Premium+** subscribers may count more than Basic Premium
- **Content format** may be weighted differently (threads, media, text)
- Only **organic** impressions count — paid promotion does not

**Implication for @igoorjm:** Tech/AI original posts, consistent cadence (3–4/day after week 2), and replies on your own posts grow the impressions that matter — not generic engagement bait.

## 90-day ramp (realistic phases)

| Phase | Focus | Impression math (rough) |
|-------|--------|-------------------------|
| **Weeks 1–4** | Dry run → 2–4 posts/day, profile + pinned tweet | Build habit; measure baseline |
| **Months 2–3** | Threads 1x/week, quote-takes, EN tech/AI niche | Need ~55K+ impressions/day avg over 90 days for 5M |
| **Ongoing** | Reply on your posts, tune watchlist, avoid spam flags | Premium viewer engagement > raw follower count |

5M in 90 days ≈ **1.67M/month** ≈ **55K/day** average organic — ambitious but achievable with daily posting + occasional viral hit in AI niche.

## Content rules aligned with monetization

- **Original commentary** — never copy watchlist posts ([posting-rules.md](posting-rules.md))
- **No engagement bait** — hurts standards compliance
- **Disclose AI-generated media** when applicable (X policy updates, e.g. conflict-related AI video)
