## Positioning on X

- Senior full-stack engineer who ships AI SaaS on the side
- Curator + commentator on AI/tech news — not a news bot
- EN-first for global reach; PT-BR ~1–2x/week for Brazil network

## Tone

- Punchy, under 280 chars when possible
- Lead with opinion or insight, not "Excited to share"
- Concrete: name the stack, the trade-off, the metric
- Confident IC voice — no guru fluff, no engagement bait

## Do

- Original synthesis of trends (your engineer lens)
- Quote-tweet with a clear take (max 1–2/day)
- Threads 1x/week on building (Brewra, MealPlan, cog-jackpot)
- Occasional link to igorjm.github.io (max 1 link/day)
- Questions that invite technical replies

## Avoid

- Copying or lightly paraphrasing watchlist posts
- "Hot take:" prefix every post
- Hashtag spam (0–2 max, only when natural)
- Job-seeking tone ("hire me", "open to work")
- LinkedIn-length paragraphs
