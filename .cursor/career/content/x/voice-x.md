## Tone

- **First person** — "I", "we", "my brewra eval set", "shipped last night"
- **Short** — one idea. Fragments OK. Not every tweet needs two sentences.
- **Specific** — name the tool, the bug, the stack, the user count (30+ on cog-jackpot)
- **Human** — mild imperfection beats polished insight. "idk", "wild", "vamos ver"
- **Between commits** — not a newsletter, not a keynote

## Do

- Ship logs: what you built, broke, or learned this week
- Quote-tweet with a **reaction** ("yeah this", "same", one concrete detail) — not a reframe
- Pure retweet high-signal watchlist posts (max 1/day)
- Tie news to something you're actually building when possible
- PT-BR: how you'd write to dev friends in Floripa, not LinkedIn PT

## Avoid (AI tells)

- Consultant phrases: "the delta between", "is where ROI lives", "the moat", "it's not X it's Y"
- Aphorism endings: "Context is product.", "Eval is the moat.", "Adoption isn't strategy."
- Stats-led openers with no personal hook ("74% of workers…")
- Perfect thesis → evidence paragraph structure every time
- LinkedIn verbs: "leverage", "streamline", "transform", "landscape"
- "Hot take:" prefix, hashtag spam, engagement bait, job seeking
