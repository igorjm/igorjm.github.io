🇺🇸 30+ users on an internal app taught me three things enterprise backlogs rarely surface:

1. Adoption beats features — people used the pool app because it saved friction every Friday, not because it was clever.
2. Boring tech wins — Next.js + TS + Vercel meant I spent time on UX, not infra archaeology.

Senior IC isn't only system design interviews. It's knowing what to build, what to cut, and when "done" means someone else relies on it weekly.
